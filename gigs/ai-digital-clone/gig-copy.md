## FAQ

1. **Do I need to speak on camera or can I keep my voice private?**
   You choose. In Basic and Standard your clone can speak using a standard TTS voice, so your real voice is never used. If you want your clone to sound exactly like you, add voice cloning (included in Premium, available as an upgrade on other packages) — I select the best available voice model for your language.

2. **Can you clone someone else's face or voice, like a celebrity or a colleague?**
   No. This service only clones your own face and voice, or the face/voice of someone who has given documented consent to be cloned. This is required by Fiverr's AI content policies and is non-negotiable — by ordering, you confirm you are the person appearing in the source video (or hold that person's explicit consent).

3. **What do I need to send you to get started?**
   Just one video of yourself, 2-5 minutes long, recorded following a simple technical guide I send you before you start filming (camera, lighting, background, framing). That single video is enough to build your full digital clone.

4. **How many languages can my clone speak?**
   As many as you need — this is a fully multilingual service. Just tell me your target language(s) and I'll match the best available voice model for that language, whether it's for one video or a full multi-language rollout.

5. **Can I use the final videos for commercial purposes (ads, courses, paid content)?**
   Basic and Standard are intended for personal/social use. If you need full commercial usage rights (ads, paid courses, client work), choose the Premium package, which includes a commercial usage license.

6. **What happens to my voice/face data after the project is done?**
   Your source files are used only to produce your ordered deliverables and are not reused for any other project. Voice model data is handled following platform privacy guidelines and is not shared with or applied to any other client.
